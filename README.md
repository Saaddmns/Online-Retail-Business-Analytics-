# Online-Retail-Business-Analytics-
Business analytics pipeline on the Online Retail II dataset   EDA, hypothesis testing, regression, and RFM customer segmentation
A full business analytics pipeline — data cleaning, exploratory analysis, statistical hypothesis testing, regression modeling, and customer segmentation — built on a real-world e-commerce transaction dataset.
This project analyzes over 1 million real transactions from a UK-based online gift retailer (Dec 2009 – Dec 2011) to answer core business questions: Who are our best customers? When do we sell the most? Which markets and products drive revenue? The analysis follows a complete pipeline from raw data to actionable business recommendations.
